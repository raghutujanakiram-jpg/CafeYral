Our Story

A café built for bright moods and unhurried mornings

YRAL Food & Beverage sits behind a striking wood‑and‑glass façade — three floors of warm timber, tall windows and quiet corners designed for coffee that lingers and conversations that don't rush.

From hand‑pulled gelatos to slow‑built soups, wood‑fired quesadillas and our house‑famous Mutton Marag, every plate leaving the pass is made to order, plated with care, and served the moment it's ready.
